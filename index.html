Cart volume & the journey milestones

  • Cart-King, Coffee Cart Startup Costs — Financial Guide 2025 — new operators 8–12 drinks/hr peak; ~12 drinks/day break-even; 42/78/130 drinks/day scenario models.
  • CoffeeShopStartups, How many cups does a coffee truck serve? — 70–150 cups/day regular spots; ~24–30 lattes/hr per group head (the one-person ceiling); events 150–500.
  • BizzOnWheels, How to start a coffee cart business — 80–100 drinks per peak window, established carts.
  • BaristaLife, Mobile espresso cart profits — 50–150 customers/day; $150–300/day new carts, $200–800/day established.
  • VettedBiz, Coffee cart business — first-year gross $30k–80k.

Drink & ticket prices (2025–26)

  • Toast POS data, Coffee pricing report — US average latte $5.60, +3.8%/yr.
  • Toast POS data, Coffee prices by state — California ~26% above national average.
  • Axios SF, SF coffee prices, Sept 2025 — SF median coffee $4.92, +9.4%/yr → SF latte ≈ $6.25–6.75 in 2026.
  • Fresh Cup, What does a latte cost? — state-by-state latte baselines.
  • Dojo Business, Coffee shop average ticket — ticket runs ~1.15–1.25× drink price with food attach.

How long carts last (used to set the prices)

  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Business Employment Dynamics — establishment survival: food service ~83% yr 1, ~58% yr 5 (better than the all-industry average).
  • LendingTree, analysis of BLS business survival data — corroborating 1/3/5-yr survival computations.
  • Luo & Stark (2014), UC Berkeley/BLS restaurant-mortality study — only ~17% of restaurants fail in year 1; median small-restaurant lifespan ~3.75–4.5 yrs. (The viral “60% of food trucks fail” stat has no primary source — we don't use it.)
  • Our blended curve for a one-person cart: 78% / 67% / 57% / 50% / 44% surviving years 1–5.

  • Crest Infosystems, Cost of POS software development — basic $30,000–$50,000; advanced $60,000–$100,000.
